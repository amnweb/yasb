<h2 align="center">Welcome to the YASB wiki!</h2>

YASB is a status bar for Windows 10 and 11. The bar and its widgets are QML files, and Python services under them
read your PC: the CPU, the battery, what's playing, your windows and more. You change a widget by setting its
options in a QML file, and you write your own widget the same way.

## Start here

- [Installation](./Installation): install YASB and run the setup.
- [Getting started](./Getting-Started): the files the setup writes, and how YASB picks up your changes.

## Customize

- [Configuration](./Configuration): `config.yaml`, with your bars, their screens and their widgets.
- [Widgets](./Widgets): add YASB's widgets to a bar and change their options.
- [Themes](./Themes): the colors and fonts of the bar, its widgets, popups and menus.
- [Layouts](./Layouts): the bar's own look, such as a full bar, a floating bar or islands.
- [Addons](./Addons): widgets, themes, layouts and configs made by others, and how to share your own.
